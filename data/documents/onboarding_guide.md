# Acme Corp - New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day

IT provisions your laptop, email account, and building access badge
roughly three business days before your start date. You will
receive a welcome email with a checklist and a link to set up
multi-factor authentication for your new account.

## First Week

Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy who is not your direct
manager, so you have someone informal to ask questions. Expect
introductory meetings with your immediate team, a short walkthrough
of internal tools, and a session with HR covering benefits
enrollment deadlines.

## Equipment and Software Requests

Standard equipment (laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse) is issued
automatically. Anything beyond the standard kit, such as a second
monitor or specialized software licenses, requires manager approval
through the IT request portal.

## Thirty and Sixty Day Check-ins

Your manager and an HR partner schedule short check-in conversations
at the 30-day and 60-day marks to see how the transition is going
and to surface any blockers early, ahead of the formal probation
review.

## Where to Find Help

The internal knowledge base under "IT Help" and "HR Policies"
answers most common questions. For anything urgent, the IT service
desk and the HR mailbox are both reachable through the intranet
homepage.
