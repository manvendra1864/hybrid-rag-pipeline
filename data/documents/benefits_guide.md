# Acme Corp - Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance

Acme Corp covers 80% of the monthly premium for the base medical
plan and 100% for preventive care visits. Dental and vision are
optional add-ons that employees can select during open enrollment
in November. Coverage for a spouse or dependents can be added at
any time within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage
or the birth of a child.

## Retirement Savings

The company matches employee 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar
up to 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately,
so there is no waiting period before the matched funds belong to
the employee. Contribution elections can be changed at any time
through the retirement plan provider's website.

## Wellness Stipend

Employees can claim up to $50 per month toward a gym membership,
fitness class, or mental health app subscription. Receipts must be
submitted through the expense portal within 60 days of purchase.
The stipend does not roll over between months.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave, and
secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave can be taken in a single
continuous block or split into two blocks within the first year
after the child arrives, subject to manager coordination.
