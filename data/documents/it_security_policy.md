# Acme Corp - IT Security Policy

## Policy IT-115: Acceptable Use Policy

Company laptops, phones, and cloud accounts are provided for
business use. Limited personal use is permitted as long as it does
not interfere with work, consume excessive bandwidth, or involve
illegal content. Installing unapproved software that requires
administrator rights must go through the IT service desk.

## Policy IT-131: Password and Authentication Standards

Passwords for company systems must be at least 12 characters long
and cannot reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, and any system that
stores customer data. Shared logins are not permitted under any
circumstance; each person must have their own account so actions
can be traced back to an individual.

## Policy IT-140: Remote Access and VPN

Connecting to internal systems from outside the office network
requires the company VPN client. Split tunneling is disabled by
default. Access logs are retained for 180 days for security audit
purposes. Employees traveling internationally should notify IT
before departure, since some countries restrict VPN usage.

## Policy IT-152: Data Classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Customer personal information and financial records are always
Restricted and may only be stored in approved systems with
encryption at rest. Sending Restricted data over personal email or
consumer file-sharing tools is a policy violation and must be
reported as a security incident.

## Policy IT-168: Incident Reporting

Any suspected phishing email, lost device, or unauthorized access
should be reported to security@acmecorp.example within one hour of
discovery. Do not attempt to investigate a suspected compromise on
your own machine before contacting the security team.
