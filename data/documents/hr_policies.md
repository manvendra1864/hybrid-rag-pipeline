# Acme Corp - HR Policies

## Policy HR-204: Remote Work Policy

Employees may perform their duties away from the primary office on a
recurring or occasional basis, subject to manager approval. Staff who
split their week between the office and another location must be
reachable during core hours (10:00-16:00 local time) and must attend
any meeting flagged as "in-person required" at least once per quarter.
Equipment provided by the company (laptop, monitor, headset) may be
used at the alternate location, but employees are responsible for a
secure and reasonably quiet working environment.

Requests to work from a different city or country for more than two
consecutive weeks must be submitted to HR at least ten business days
in advance, since payroll and tax residency rules can be affected.

## Policy HR-118: Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month,
capped at 21 days per calendar year. Unused time above 5 days does
not roll over into the next year. PTO requests should be submitted
through the HR portal at least 3 business days before the requested
date, except in cases of illness or emergency.

## Policy HR-142: Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and
partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation
against anyone who reports a concern in good faith are strictly
prohibited. Violations should be reported to HR or through the
anonymous ethics hotline listed on the intranet homepage.

## Policy HR-160: Probation and Performance Reviews

New hires undergo a 90-day probationary period during which their
manager and an HR partner conduct a 30-day and a 60-day check-in.
Formal performance reviews for all staff occur twice a year, in
March and September, and are used as an input to compensation
adjustments.
